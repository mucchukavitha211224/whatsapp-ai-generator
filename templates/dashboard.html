Twilio Webhook Setup

To wire up the Twilio WhatsApp Sandbox to your local machine, run Ngrok in your terminal:

ngrok http 5000

Copy the generated HTTPS URL, append /webhook to the end, and paste it into the **Twilio Sandbox Settings** on Twilio Console:

https://your-id.ngrok-free.app/webhook
